Contract specs

Tick size and tick value fill in automatically from the symbol root — ESZ5, /MNQ, MNQM6 and NQ all resolve. Anything not listed defaults to 1 point = 1 unit, and you can override tick size and point value on any trade.

About & disclaimers

Not an accounting record. P&L is computed from the executions you enter and from a built-in contract spec table that may be incomplete or out of date; exchanges change contract specifications. Reconcile against your broker statements, and use your broker’s official records for taxes.
No warranty. Provided as-is under the MIT License, with no warranty of any kind. Trading futures and other leveraged instruments involves substantial risk of loss. Past results shown in this journal do not predict future performance.
Offline is enforced, not just promised. This file carries a Content Security Policy that instructs your browser to refuse every outbound network request and to block loading any script, image, style or font from the internet. It is the second line of the file’s source, so you can verify it yourself, and you can confirm nothing is sent by opening your browser’s developer tools (F12) and watching the Network tab while you use the app. Because of this, the app cannot download market data; price bars for replay must be imported from a CSV.
Your data is local and fragile. Everything is stored in this browser on this computer. Nothing is transmitted anywhere — there is no telemetry, no analytics, no account. Clearing browser data, switching browsers or changing Windows profiles will make the journal appear empty. Treat the JSON backup as the real record.
